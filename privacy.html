Sera ya Faragha (Kiswahili)

Sera hii inaeleza jinsi programu ya Jengo Calc (com.kivumbi.jengocalc), inayotolewa na Kivumbi ("sisi"), inavyoshughulikia taarifa zako. Jengo Calc ni kikokotoo cha ujenzi (BOQ, ngazi, uzio, tanki la maji, umeme n.k.) kwa watumiaji wa Afrika Mashariki.

1. Muhtasari

  • Hakuna akaunti wala usajili.
  • Miradi na hesabu zako zinahifadhiwa kwenye simu yako tu. Hatuzitumi kwa seva zetu.
  • Hatuuzi wala hatushiriki data yako kwa matangazo.
  • Kipengele cha AI ni cha hiari. Kikitumika, data hutumwa moja kwa moja kutoka simu yako kwenda Anthropic.

2. Taarifa tunazoshughulikia

  • Data ya miradi na hesabu: majina ya miradi, vipimo, orodha ya vifaa (BOQ), bei na mipangilio. Zinahifadhiwa kwenye hifadhi ya ndani ya programu (localStorage) kwenye kifaa chako. Tunazipata? Hapana.
  • Funguo ya API ya Anthropic (hiari): ukiweka funguo yako mwenyewe, inahifadhiwa kwenye kifaa chako tu na inatumika kuthibitisha maombi ya AI.
  • Faili unazochagua: picha au PDF ya ramani ya jengo (kwa AI Vision) na faili za miradi (JSON) unazoingiza. Zinasomwa kwenye kifaa chako.
  • Jina la mji/eneo la hali ya hewa: kipengele cha hali ya hewa hutuma jina la mji kwa huduma ya wttr.in kupata utabiri. Programu haitumii GPS kupata eneo lako halisi.
  • Arifa: ukiruhusu, programu inaonyesha vikumbusho ulivyoweka. Vikumbusho vinatengenezwa kwenye kifaa chako.

3. Kipengele cha AI (hiari)

Ukitumia msaidizi wa AI au uchambuzi wa ramani ya jengo, maandishi unayoandika na picha/PDF unayochagua hutumwa kwa Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) kwa kutumia funguo ya API yako. Sisi hatuoni wala hatuhifadhi data hii. Anthropic huishughulikia kwa mujibu wa sera yao: anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Usitume taarifa nyeti za kibinafsi kupitia kipengele hiki. Usipotumia AI, hakuna data inayotumwa.

4. Huduma za wahusika wengine

Programu inaweza kupakia rasilimali kutoka mtandaoni zinapohitajika: Google Fonts (fonti), maktaba ya jsPDF kutoka cdnjs/jsDelivr/unpkg (kutengeneza PDF), na wttr.in (hali ya hewa). Huduma hizi zinaweza kupokea anwani yako ya IP kama sehemu ya ombi la kawaida la mtandao. Pia viungo vya WhatsApp (wa.me) na Ramani za Google vinafunguka nje ya programu pale unapovibonyeza. Hatuna matangazo wala zana za uchanganuzi (analytics) ndani ya programu.

5. Ruhusa za programu

  • Intaneti / hali ya mtandao: kwa AI, hali ya hewa na kupakia fonti/maktaba.
  • Arifa: kwa vikumbusho vyako.
  • Hifadhi/faili: kuhifadhi PDF na kuingiza/kutoa faili za miradi kupitia kiteuzi cha mfumo.

6. Uhifadhi na ufutaji wa data

Data yote iko kwenye kifaa chako. Unaweza kuifuta wakati wowote kwa kufuta miradi ndani ya programu, kufuta data ya programu (Mipangilio ya simu → Programu → Jengo Calc → Hifadhi → Futa data), au kuiondoa programu. Kwa kuwa hatuna nakala ya data yako, hakuna kitu cha kufutwa kwenye seva zetu.

7. Usalama

Tunatumia muunganisho salama (HTTPS) kwa maombi yote ya mtandaoni. Hata hivyo, hakuna njia ya uhifadhi au usafirishaji iliyo salama kwa 100%. Linda kifaa chako na funguo yako ya API.

8. Watoto

Programu haielekezwi kwa watoto chini ya miaka 13 na hatukusanyi kwa makusudi taarifa za watoto.

9. Mabadiliko ya sera

Tukibadilisha sera hii, tutasasisha tarehe iliyo juu ya ukurasa huu. Mabadiliko makubwa yatatangazwa ndani ya programu au kwenye ukurasa wa Google Play.

Privacy Policy (English)

This policy explains how the Jengo Calc app (com.kivumbi.jengocalc), provided by Kivumbi ("we"), handles your information. Jengo Calc is a construction calculator (BOQ, stairs, fencing, water tank, electrical and more) for East African users.

1. Summary

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • Your projects and calculations are stored on your device only. We do not send them to our servers.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • The AI feature is optional. If you use it, data goes directly from your device to Anthropic.

2. Information we handle

  • Project and calculation data: project names, measurements, bills of quantities, prices and settings. Stored in the app's local storage (localStorage) on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Anthropic API key (optional): if you enter your own key, it is stored on your device only and used to authenticate your AI requests.
  • Files you choose: floor-plan images or PDFs (for AI Vision) and project files (JSON) you import. These are read on your device.
  • City name for weather: the weather feature sends a city name to wttr.in to fetch a forecast. The app does not use GPS to determine your precise location.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the app shows reminders you create. Reminders are generated on your device.

3. AI feature (optional)

If you use the AI assistant or floor-plan analysis, the text you type and the images/PDFs you select are sent to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) using your own API key. We do not see or store this data. Anthropic handles it under its own policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through this feature. If you do not use AI, no such data is sent.

4. Third-party services

The app may load online resources when needed: Google Fonts (fonts), the jsPDF library from cdnjs/jsDelivr/unpkg (PDF generation), and wttr.in (weather). These services may receive your IP address as part of a normal network request. WhatsApp (wa.me) and Google Maps links open outside the app when you tap them. The app contains no ads and no analytics tools.

5. App permissions

  • Internet / network state: for AI, weather and loading fonts/libraries.
  • Notifications: for your reminders.
  • Storage/files: to save PDFs and import/export project files through the system file picker.

6. Retention and deletion

All data is on your device. You can delete it at any time by removing projects inside the app, clearing app data (phone Settings → Apps → Jengo Calc → Storage → Clear data), or uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing to delete on our servers.

7. Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS) for all online requests. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so please protect your device and your API key.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on the Google Play listing.
